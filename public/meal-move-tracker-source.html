Metabolic MannaWhy this works
Two moves, one week

Same food.
Smaller spike.

Most metabolic advice asks you to overhaul what you eat. The two moves in this tracker ask something simpler — change the order you eat in, and add a short walk after the meal. Same food. Same calories. Different result in published trials.

Neither move requires a continuous glucose monitor, a supplement, or a religion. They are two of the cleanest, lowest-friction findings in the post-meal glucose literature, and they are what this tracker is built around.

Move 01 · Sequencing

Vegetables and protein before carbs.

In a 2019 trial of adults with prediabetes (Shukla et al.), eating vegetables and protein first and saving carbohydrates for last reduced the incremental glucose peak by up to 54%. A 2015 study in adults with type 2 diabetes showed approximately a 29% reduction at 30 minutes.

Shukla 2015 · Shukla 2019
Move 02 · Walking

A 10-minute walk within fifteen minutes.

In a 2016 trial of 41 adults with type 2 diabetes (Reynolds et al.), three 10-minute post-meal walks lowered post-meal glucose by ~12% overall and ~22% after dinner. A 2023 meta-analysis (Engeroff et al.) found post-meal exercise is significantly more effective than pre-meal exercise.

Reynolds 2016 · Engeroff 2023
Honest expectations

The largest effects in these studies were observed in adults with type 2 diabetes or prediabetes. Healthy adults transiently spike above 140 mg/dL after typical meals — that's normal physiology (Shah 2019). Individual responses vary. If you have diabetes, are on glucose-affecting medication, or have any condition involving blood sugar, talk to your healthcare provider before changing how you eat or exercise.

Metabolic MannaHow to use this
A short manual

Five things, then start.

i

Each day has three meal slots.

Use them however your day actually runs — three meals, two meals plus a snack, or one big meal. Empty slots are fine.

ii

Mark "sequenced ✓" if vegetables or protein came first.

Salad before pasta. Eggs before toast. Soup before sandwich. The first few bites being non-carb is what counts.

iii

Mark "walked ✓" if you walked 10+ minutes after.

Within about 15 minutes of finishing the meal is the window the literature supports most directly. A loop around the block counts.

iv

Write one win at the end of the day.

Not a graded report. Just a sentence — what worked, what surprised you, or what you noticed. The reflection matters more than the checks.

v

Track the pattern. Don't chase the number.

This is not a glucose monitor and the verses on each page are not a sermon. The body is a gift to steward, not a project to optimize.
